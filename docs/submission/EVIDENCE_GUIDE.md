# Development conversation history bundle

Required: **chat history and at least 3 screenshots** from actual CodeBuddy,
WorkBuddy or Miora development sessions, combined into **one supported file ≤10 MB**.
Proposed output: `assets/development-history.pdf`; confirm PDF support in the form.
The supplied current form explicitly includes Miora. Do not infer that source
commits or AI-generated artwork alone replace the chat/screenshots requirement.

## Collection instructions

The user retrieves genuine sessions. Useful candidates already evidenced by
repository history: CodeBuddy UI redesign, Miora original illustration creation,
and Miora breathing illustration iteration. Do not claim a specific chat is
available until the original is supplied. Codex/Claude logs alone do not meet
the named-tool requirement.

Preserve raw originals in ignored `private/`, not Git. Capture at least 3 distinct,
legible screenshots showing actual development requests/responses. Keep enough
tool/session context to establish authenticity and task; do not fabricate dates,
reconstruct a missing conversation as an original, or create screenshots from
a made-up chat interface.

## Single-file layout

1. Cover/index: project, tools actually used, evidence dates and related commits.
2. Actual chat-history export or selected transcript with clear excerpts/omissions.
3. At least 3 screenshot pages, each with genuine tool, session/task, date if
   visible, and concise explanation of what was built or revised.
4. Provenance table mapping entries to supplied originals and implementation/assets.

Suggested screenshot coverage: initial task/boundaries → generated result →
review/iteration. It need not be three different tools. Verify the actual form
before relying on any organizer-specific interpretation.

## Redaction and verification

Remove keys, admin tokens, account credentials, cookies, real patient text,
private unrelated chats and sensitive provider errors. Mark redactions honestly;
retain evidence of the actual task/tool. Screenshots may be cropped/compressed
for privacy/readability, but must not be edited to invent dialogue or success.
Do not include the user-supplied Tencent-internal operation-guide PDF.

Check text/screenshot readability at normal zoom, screenshot count≥3, chat
history present, supported format and file < 10,000,000 bytes. Only sanitized,
user-approved public bundle may be committed. Any original chat sharing or
publication needs the user's permission.
