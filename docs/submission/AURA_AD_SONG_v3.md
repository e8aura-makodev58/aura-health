## Musical direction

A warm, polished late-1970s soft-rock song about everyday life, keeping the small
details together, and having space to tell your own story. Intimate verses open
into an easy, memorable chorus. Hopeful without demanding that the listener
feel happy. The brand appears in the chorus and closing signature.

Pronounce **AURA** as **“OR-uh”**, two syllables. Sing “Health” normally. The lyrics write it
as **Aura** so it is sung as a word; on-screen titles keep **AURA Health**.

Target: about **96 BPM** (Suno range 92–104), **4/4**. The 68-bar arrangement below
lasts about **2:50** at 96 BPM (2:37 at 104, 2:57 at 92). Generated phrasing may change the
duration; these timings are an arrangement brief, not a guaranteed output length.
