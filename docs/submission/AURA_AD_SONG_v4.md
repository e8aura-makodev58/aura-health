# AURA Health — Room for Your Day (v4)

Original lyrics and Suno prompt for the AURA Health video advertisement. Creative draft; audio
has not been generated.

**v4 (2026-10-14, Claude, user-directed): lyrics rewritten to fit the prompt.** Same style prompt
as v3 (the user's LOCI prompt adapted to Aura, **92–104 BPM**). Every verse, pre-chorus and bridge
line is now 7 syllables with the stress on the beat (DA da DA da DA da DA, one line per bar),
in true rhyming couplets. The chorus follows the LOCI hook shape: the name, then two parallel
halves the harmonies can answer ("Aura, room for your day / room for your own way"). Story and
meaning per section are kept; the outro still ends on "Room for your day". Earlier versions:
v3, v2 (this folder) and Codex's original (`improve/feature-codex`, commit `7ab3502`).

## Suno style prompt — paste into **Style**

```text
Late-1970s soft rock with a warm, polished analog studio sound. Smooth electric piano, clean electric guitars, gentle acoustic guitar, melodic bass, restrained live drums, subtle string or synth pads, and rich vocal harmonies. Tempo around 92–104 BPM. Relaxed but uplifting groove, with a flowing verse and a strong melodic chorus that feels nostalgic, optimistic, and memorable. The arrangement should gradually open up, with tasteful guitar fills and layered harmonies rather than heavy production. Lead vocal should be warm, natural, expressive, and conversational, with clear pronunciation. Add soft multi-part backing vocals in the chorus, especially around the phrase "Aura, room for your day." Sing Aura as "OR-uh". Original melody; no imitation of a particular artist.
```
