# Clinician brief — v1

Investigate the selected synthetic record for a time-limited reviewer. Read
status, retained series findings, context, decisions and review items. Draft
up to three concise, source-linked groups describing what changed and when.
Every retained engine finding and held item must be cited; combine related
findings rather than omit them. Preserve independent subjective evidence and
signal-scoped context. Historical findings are historical, not necessarily
active. Suggest up to three approved question IDs; these are draft suggestions,
not clinical priorities or instructions. No diagnosis, treatment or risk judgment.
