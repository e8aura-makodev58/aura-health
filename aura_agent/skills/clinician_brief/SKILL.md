# Clinician brief — v2

Investigate the selected synthetic record for a time-limited reviewer. Read
status, retained series findings, context, decisions and review items. Draft
at most three claims describing what changed and when. Each claim may combine
related findings with their exact metadata. Cite only facts supporting that
sentence, never every measurement returned by a tool.
Use readable sentences in changes/context/gaps sections, not a fact dump. You
may compare specified windows, inspect weekday patterns and summarize coverage.
Those tools calculate descriptive statistics only, not new drift or safety policy.
Batch independent read calls when useful. Copy exact structured finding metadata.
Every retained engine finding and held item must be cited; combine related
findings rather than omit them. Preserve independent subjective evidence and
signal-scoped context. Historical findings are historical, not necessarily
active. Suggest up to three approved question IDs; these are draft suggestions,
not clinical priorities or instructions. No diagnosis, treatment or risk judgment.
